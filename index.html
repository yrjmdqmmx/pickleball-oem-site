FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

Something else on your mind? Ask us in the quote form.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ 300 pcs. Logo, graphics, colors, grip and packaging can be customized.

What can I customize?

Logo, graphics, colors, grip and packaging can be customized. Logo: UV print, up to 2 colours. Standard edge guard; printed/electroplated edge guard available at extra cost. Packaging: OPP bag; custom colour box available (quoted after design). Packing: up to 20 pcs per carton with colour box. Weight: 225 ± 5 g or 230 ± 5 g. Size can be customized; share your requirements in the inquiry.

Can I test a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes, samples take about 12 days, up to 10 pcs. Sample fee on request. Tell us what you need in the quote form to discuss sample details.

How much does a paddle cost?

Prices are available on request. Include your quantity, customization requirements and preferred trade terms when requesting a quote.

Which shipping terms do you offer?

FOB Shenzhen / CIF Rotterdam / DDP on request. Include your destination and preferred terms in the inquiry.

What is the lead time?

Bulk lead time: 35–40 days. Please include your target delivery date and destination in the inquiry so we can confirm shipping arrangements.

Is a 14 mm thickness option available?

Yes, both 16 mm and 14 mm thicknesses are available. PP honeycomb core. T700 carbon fiber face, 3K or 12K.

Are the paddles USA Pickleball approved?

Not currently. Approval is per brand and model: we can help you apply under your own brand, with testing arranged after your design is final.
