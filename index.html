FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

Something else on your mind? Ask us in the quote form.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Full OEM / private-label orders start at 100 pcs. That includes custom graphics, logo, grip, edge guard and retail box.

What can I customize?

Paddle graphics, logo, grip, edge guard and retail box. Weight and size are customizable too — ask us for the spec sheet.

Can I test a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Sampling is a standard step in our process, before design approval and mass production. Tell us what you need in the quote form and we'll confirm the sample details.

How much does a paddle cost?

Pricing depends on quantity, customization and trade term, so we don't publish a price list. Request a quote and we'll send indicative pricing for your order.

Which shipping terms do you offer?

FOB Shenzhen, CIF, or DDP to your US warehouse. Buyers in the UK and EU are welcome too — tell us your destination and we'll quote the term that suits you.

What is the lead time?

It depends on order quantity and how much customization is involved. We confirm a production and shipping timeline together with your quote.

Can you help with USA Pickleball approval?

Ask us. USAP-approval support is available on request — mention it in your inquiry and we'll explain what's involved.
