FAQ

OEM pickleball paddle questions buyers ask first.

Something else on your mind? Ask us in the quote form.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

The MOQ for bulk orders is 300 pcs.

Can I order fewer than 300 pcs?

Not as a bulk order. You can order samples first: up to 10 pcs, in about 12 days. Bulk orders start at 300 pcs.

What can I customize?

Logo, graphics, colors, grip and packaging. Logos are UV printed in up to 2 colors. The standard edge guard can be upgraded to a printed or electroplated one at extra cost. Standard packaging is an OPP bag; a custom color box is quoted after design. Size can also be customized: share your requirements in the inquiry.

Can I test a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples take about 12 days, up to 10 pcs.

What artwork files do you need?

AI, PDF, PNG or SVG. Mention your artwork in the inquiry and we'll reply with an address to send files.

Which shipping terms and ports do you offer?

FOB Shenzhen, or CIF / DDP to your port — tell us your destination.

What is the lead time?

Bulk lead time is 35–40 days. Include your target delivery date and destination in the inquiry so we can confirm shipping arrangements.

Is a 14 mm thickness option available?

Yes. Both 16 mm (≈ 0.63 in) and 14 mm (≈ 0.55 in) are available, each with a PP honeycomb core.

What is the difference between 3K and 12K?

3K and 12K describe the visible carbon weave pattern; both use T700 fiber.

Is Paddle Match the factory?

No. Paddle Match is an export team that works with a partner factory in Dongguan, Guangdong, China. We handle communication in English, specs, samples, artwork checks and shipping coordination; the factory handles production.
